How to play

Each cipher letter stands for one real letter, and the same cipher letter always means the same real letter. Click any box, then type a letter — every matching cipher letter is filled in at once. Correct guesses glow green.

WKH FDW→THE CAT

  • A one-letter word is almost always A or I.
  • Look for common short words like THE, AND and IS.
  • Stuck? Reveal word solves a short word for you; Hint reveals one letter. Both lock their letters and count as hints.
  • Open Letter frequencies below to see which cipher letters appear most often — in English those are usually E, T and A.
  • Type A–Z or use the on-screen keyboard. Backspace clears, Esc pauses.
