How to play

You're a warehouse worker. Push every crate onto a glowing target to clear the level — you can push crates, but never pull them.

  • Stand on the opposite side of a crate from where you want it to go. Crates can't pass through walls or each other.
  • Move with Arrow keys / WASD or the pad, U undoes a step, Esc pauses.
  • A green crate is home. A crate that flashes red is wedged in a corner and can't be freed — use Undo.
  • Beat your Best move count on each level. Undoing or resetting costs you a clean run.
