How to play

Fill the 5×5 grid so every row and every column spells a real five-letter word. The five words are the same read across and down, so once you know a word you can use it twice.

BEACH EXTRA ATLAS CRAFT HASTE

Row 1 is BEACH — and column 1 is BEACH too. The same goes for all five words.

  • Click a cell, then type A–Z to fill it. Arrows move, Backspace clears.
  • A row or column turns green as soon as it spells a valid word.
  • Stuck? Starting letters reveals the first letter of each word; Reveal word gives you one whole word. Both count as hints.
  • Esc pauses. The clock stops while you are paused.

Rows and columns must all spell 5-letter words. Click a cell and type; green means a word. Open “How to play” above for an example.
